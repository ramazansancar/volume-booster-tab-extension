# 👋 Sorry to see you go

**Volume Booster Tab has been removed.**

We're sorry it didn't work out. If something went wrong, telling us about it is the best way to get it fixed, for you and for everyone else.

## 🐞 Tell us what went wrong

Did a site not get louder? Did the sound crackle? Was a feature missing? Is a translation wrong?

**[Report a problem](https://github.com/ramazansancar/volume-booster-tab-extension/issues/new)**

These details help us fix it:

- Your browser and its version
- The site you were on
- What you expected to happen, and what happened instead
- What the status line at the bottom of the popup said

You need a GitHub account to open an issue. Every report gets read.

## 🔁 Changed your mind?

Install it again from your browser's store:
